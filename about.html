Who We Are · Our Programs · Our Standing

A 501(c)(3) foundation putting gardens to work for education and the environment.

Mountain and Sea Foundation is a federally recognized public charity that directly runs its own gardening, education, and environmental programs — we don't just fund other organizations, we do the work ourselves.

Our Legal Status

Verified Nonprofit Standing

Tax-Exempt Status

Mountain and Sea Foundation is recognized by the IRS as tax-exempt under Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(3), effective June 16, 2025. Donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.

EIN: 39-3607866

Foundation Type

We are classified as a private operating foundation under IRC Section 4942(j)(3) — meaning the IRS requires us to directly conduct our own charitable activities, rather than simply making grants to other organizations.

Our Mission

What We Do

Mountain and Sea Foundation connects people to nature and builds healthy, resilient communities through hands-on gardening, environmental education, and stewardship of shared green space. We advance two IRS-recognized charitable purposes: advancing education and preserving and restoring the environment.

How We're Governed

The foundation is led by its founder and a volunteer Board of Directors, who oversee our programs and finances as unpaid executive staff. We currently operate without paid staff — our leadership handles day-to-day operations directly.

Our Programs

What we actually do, in specific terms

Environmental & Garden Education

We teach children and community members about plants, ecology, and the natural sciences through hands-on lessons, partnering with schools, community groups, and garden networks to expand our educational reach.

Community Gardens

Our volunteer leadership plants, tends, and maintains public foundation gardens — restoring green space, improving local ecosystems, and giving neighbors a shared place to garden together.

Our Planty

Community Plant Marketplace

A mission-driven program of the foundation — not a separate commercial venture — where members share and sell homegrown plants, with a portion of proceeds directed back to fund our education and environmental programs.
