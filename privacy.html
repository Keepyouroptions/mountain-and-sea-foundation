Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated August 19, 2026. This page explains what we collect from visitors to mountainandseafoundation.org, why, and what choices you have.

The Short Version

Information We Collect

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, subject line, message, and an optional note about what you're interested in (teaching, volunteering, donating, or Our Planty). We don't use analytics or tracking scripts on this site, and we don't collect information automatically beyond what your browser sends to any website by default, like your general location or device type.

How the Contact Form Works

When you submit the contact form, it opens your own email application with your message already filled in — the form doesn't send anything to a server we run. That means your message travels directly from your email client to ours, the same as if you'd written us an email yourself. If your browser doesn't support this, you can copy our email address and reach us that way instead.

How We Use What You Send Us

We use the information in your message only to respond to you — whether that's answering a question, coordinating a volunteer shift, setting up a teaching partnership, or processing a donation inquiry. We don't sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Fonts and Third-Party Services

This site loads two typefaces, Fraunces and Work Sans, from Google Fonts. Loading a font this way means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which can see your IP address as part of that request. We're working on serving these fonts directly from our own site instead, which would remove that exposure. If you'd rather avoid it in the meantime, most browser privacy extensions that block third-party font or script requests will do so without affecting how the page reads.

Data Retention

Since contact form submissions go straight to our email inbox rather than a database we manage, retention follows our normal email practices — we keep messages as long as we need them to follow up with you, and delete or archive them afterward like any other correspondence.

Your Rights and Choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have from you, request that we delete a message you sent us, or ask us not to use your contact details for anything beyond replying to your original inquiry. Reach out using the details below and we'll take care of it directly — we're a small, volunteer-led organization, so you'll hear back from an actual person.

Changes to This Policy

If how we handle information changes, we'll update this page and adjust the date at the top. We'll keep the language plain and specific rather than burying changes in dense legal text.

Contact Us About Privacy

Questions about this policy, or a request related to your information, can go to hello@mountainandseafoundation.org. Mountain and Sea Foundation is a 501(c)(3) private operating foundation, EIN 39-3607866, based in Chattanooga, Tennessee.
