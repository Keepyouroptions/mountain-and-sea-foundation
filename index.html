The Heart of Our Foundation

Who We Are

Mountain and Sea Foundation is a nonprofit organization devoted to gardening, nature-inspired arts and scientific creation, connecting people deeply to the places we depend on, and fostering healthy, resilient communities for today and for tomorrow.

Learn more about us →

Why Support Us

We are an active foundation doing hands-on charity and education to build local communities to share the love of nature. We focus on measurable work: promote gardening, build community gardens, teach children about plants, arts, and science, and engage other adjacent programs.

Mission and Vision

Mission

A world where every garden is a classroom and a source of philanthropic growth, ensuring the next generation is rooted in environmental stewardship and scientific curiosity.

Vision

We envision a future where our shared efforts protect and preserve the environment, deepening people's love of nature, where we call our home.
